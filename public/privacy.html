3. Cookies

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. Your browser may also remember a few preferences on your own device, such as your last recording mode.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell or rent personal data. We use these service providers to run Madifyx Clips, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Render: hosts the application.
  • Cloudflare: stores videos and account data (R2 storage) and runs our domain's DNS.
  • Resend: sends email verification codes.
  • Stripe: processes payments and manages subscriptions.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the fonts used on our pages, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

Anyone who has a video's share link can watch that video and see its comments. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Videos and comments: until you or your workspace admin deletes them, or your account is deleted.
  • Account details: for as long as your account exists.
  • Unconfirmed sign ups: deleted automatically after 30 minutes.
  • Billing records: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.
  • Server logs: kept briefly, then deleted.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent. You can change your name and password yourself in your account settings, and delete videos at any time. For anything else, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your account's email address and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, video storage is private (videos are reached through time-limited links), and new accounts must confirm their email. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

8. Children

Madifyx Clips isn't for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date above. For important changes, we'll also let you know by email or in the app.
