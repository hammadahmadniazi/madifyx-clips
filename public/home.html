Questions

Do people need an account to watch?

No. Anyone with the link can watch, react and comment right in their browser. Only the people recording need an account.

Can I record on my phone?

Yes, with your phone's front or back camera, or by uploading a video you've already filmed. Phone browsers don't allow screen recording, so screen recordings are made on a computer.

What happens when I reach the Free limits?

Your videos stay exactly where they are. To record more, delete older videos or upgrade to Pro for unlimited videos and recording time.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Click Manage billing in your workspace settings to change your card or cancel. If you cancel, your workspace goes back to Free and nothing is deleted.

Who can see my videos?

Only people you send a link to. Each workspace is private, and teammates only see each other's videos when an admin chooses to.
